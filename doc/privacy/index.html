1. Introduction

Welcome to BRYT Pay.

BRYT Pay is a digital financial and utility services platform operated by BRYT Digital Services, a business registered in Nigeria.

We are committed to respecting your privacy, safeguarding your personal information, and maintaining transparency about how your information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected.

This Privacy Policy explains our data-handling practices when you access or use BRYT Pay through our website, Android mobile application, or related services.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and other applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

By using BRYT Pay, you acknowledge that you have been informed of the data-processing practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

2. Services Covered by This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the following BRYT Pay services:

  • Airtime purchases and mobile data subscriptions.
  • Television subscription payments.
  • Educational examination PINs and related services.
  • JAMB services, including admission letters, result printing, document reprinting, and admission status checks.
  • NIN slip requests and related document-processing services.
  • Wallet funding, wallet balance management, and transaction history.
  • Transfers from BRYT Pay wallets to supported Nigerian bank accounts.
  • Customer support, account management, and other services made available through the platform.

This policy also applies to future services unless a separate privacy notice is provided.

3. Information We Collect

We collect personal information reasonably necessary to operate our platform, process transactions, provide customer support, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3.1 Account Information

When you register or use BRYT Pay, we may collect:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Account ID or unique user identifier.
  • Authentication information.
  • Account creation date and account status.

Passwords are managed through secure authentication mechanisms. We do not require users to disclose their passwords to customer support.

3.2 Wallet and Transaction Information

When you fund your wallet, purchase services, or initiate transfers, we may process:

  • Wallet balance and funding history.
  • Transaction amounts and references.
  • Transaction dates and timestamps.
  • Payment methods and payment statuses.
  • Service descriptions and purchase records.
  • Refund and reversal information.
  • Information required for transaction verification and dispute resolution.

3.3 Bank Transfer Information

When you transfer money from your BRYT Pay wallet to a supported bank account, we may collect and process:

  • Recipient bank name.
  • Recipient account number.
  • Recipient account name.
  • Transfer amount.
  • Transfer reference.
  • Transfer status and related transaction records.
  • Other information necessary to verify, authorize, or complete the transfer.

Relevant information may be transmitted securely to our payment-processing and banking partners to execute the transaction.

We do not sell your bank account information or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

3.4 Airtime, Data, and Television Services

Depending on the service requested, we may collect:

  • Recipient mobile phone numbers.
  • Selected network providers.
  • Data or airtime purchase details.
  • Television provider details.
  • Smartcard numbers or decoder account identifiers.
  • Subscription packages and transaction records.

3.5 JAMB and Educational Services

When you request JAMB or educational services, we may collect:

  • Candidate full name.
  • JAMB registration number.
  • Examination year.
  • Email address.
  • Contact or WhatsApp number.
  • Examination or registration details necessary to fulfil the request.
  • Documents generated or processed for delivery.

This information is used to process your requested service and provide the resulting document or confirmation.

3.6 NIN Services

When you request an NIN-related service, we may process:

  • National Identification Number, where required.
  • Personal details necessary to locate or process the requested record.
  • Contact information.
  • Selected slip or document type.
  • Information necessary to complete and deliver the request.

NIN-related information is used only for authorized service fulfilment, verification where applicable, customer support, and legally required recordkeeping.

We restrict access to sensitive identity information to authorized personnel and relevant service providers.

3.7 Technical and Usage Information

When you access BRYT Pay through our website or Android application, we may process technical information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and operating system.
  • Device type.
  • Session information.
  • Login activity.
  • Security and error logs.
  • Cookies and local storage information.

Additional technical information may be processed by our hosting, security, or application service providers where necessary to operate and protect the platform.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for legitimate and specified purposes, including:

  1. Creating and managing your BRYT Pay account.
  2. Authenticating users and maintaining secure login sessions.
  3. Funding wallets and maintaining accurate wallet balances.
  4. Processing bank transfers and digital service purchases.

  6. Sending transaction confirmations, receipts, and important service notifications.
  7. Providing customer support and resolving complaints.
  8. Detecting fraud, unauthorized transactions, and misuse.
  9. Processing refunds, reversals, and disputed transactions.
  10. Improving platform performance, reliability, and user experience.
  11. Maintaining records required by applicable laws and regulations.
  12. Communicating service updates and promotional information where permitted by law.

We do not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those described in this policy without an appropriate legal basis.

Depending on the nature of the information and service, we may process personal data based on:

Contractual necessity: To provide services requested by users, maintain accounts, and process transactions.

Legal obligations: To comply with applicable financial, tax, data protection, fraud prevention, and other regulatory requirements.

6. Financial Services, Wallet Operations and Banking Partners

BRYT Pay is a digital financial and utility services platform operated by BRYT Digital Services, a CAC-registered business in Nigeria.

BRYT Pay integrates with third-party payment infrastructure providers, including PayVessel, to facilitate supported financial transactions and wallet-to-bank transfers.

Depending on the service, these integrations may enable users to fund their wallets, manage transaction records, and transfer funds to supported Nigerian bank accounts.

To process these transactions, BRYT Pay may securely share relevant information with PayVessel and its applicable banking or payment-processing partners, including:

  • Customer identification and account information.
  • Beneficiary bank names and account numbers.
  • Recipient account names.
  • Transaction amounts and references.
  • Payment and transfer statuses.
  • Information required for transaction verification, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

BRYT Pay does not operate as an independently licensed bank or deposit-taking institution.

Financial transactions are processed through the relevant third-party payment infrastructure and banking arrangements, subject to their applicable terms, licensing obligations, and regulatory requirements.

Customer funds are handled according to the applicable safeguarding and settlement arrangements of the financial institutions involved. Any deposit insurance or other protection applies only where expressly provided under the relevant institution's arrangements and applicable law.

BRYT Pay is committed to maintaining accurate transaction records, protecting customer information, and assisting users with transaction disputes, failed transfers, refunds, and other payment-related concerns.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • Whether an account remains active.
  • Whether a transaction is pending or disputed.
  • Financial recordkeeping obligations.
  • Fraud prevention and security requirements.
  • Applicable legal or regulatory retention periods.
  • The need to resolve customer complaints or enforce legal rights.

When personal information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.

Certain transaction and financial records may be retained after account deletion where required for legal, regulatory, accounting, security, or fraud prevention purposes.

Such retained records will not be used to reactivate a deleted account or for unrelated marketing purposes.

11. Account Deletion and Data Removal

BRYT Pay respects users' rights to request the deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

Users can initiate an account-deletion request by contacting our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to: brytpay@gmail.com

Use the subject: BRYT Pay — Account Deletion Request

Include the following details:

  1. Your BRYT Pay Account ID.
  2. Your registered email address.
  3. Your reason for requesting account deletion.

Providing a reason helps us understand users' experiences and improve our services. It is optional and will not determine whether an otherwise valid deletion request is accepted.

For account security, we may verify that the request comes from the legitimate account holder before proceeding.

Processing Time

Verified account-deletion requests will be processed within three (3) days of successful verification, subject to applicable legal obligations and any outstanding transaction or account-security issues.

We will inform users if additional steps or legally required retention obligations affect their request.

What Happens After Account Deletion?

Following successful processing:

  • The BRYT Pay account will be permanently closed.
  • Account authentication access will be removed.
  • Personal profile information that is no longer required will be deleted.
  • Associated service information and documents will be deleted where no longer required.
  • Information held by relevant service providers will be addressed in accordance with applicable agreements and legal requirements.
  • Financial and transaction records required by law may be retained for the applicable retention period.

Account deletion is not the same as temporary suspension or deactivation.

Important Information About Wallet Balances

Users should resolve pending transactions and withdraw or otherwise settle any available wallet balance before account deletion.

Where funds remain or transactions are unresolved, our support team will provide appropriate assistance before completing account closure.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel a completed transfer or reverse a successfully processed transaction.

Account Deletion Information Page

Users may also review the account-deletion procedure at: https://brytpay.name.ng/doc/delete-account/
