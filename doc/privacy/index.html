Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At BRYT Pay, a product of BRYT Technologies, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data securely and transparently. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect and process specific types of information:

Account Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Authentication credentials (securely hashed)

Transaction Information

  • Transaction references and IDs
  • Service purchased and the amount
  • Transaction status (Pending, Completed, Failed)
  • Dates and timestamps of your activities

Wallet Information

  • Wallet balance and funding records
  • Payment references from third-party processors (e.g., Flutterwave)

Service-Specific Information

Depending on the service you purchase, we may collect:

  • Phone numbers (for Airtime/Data)
  • Smartcard or Account numbers (for TV subscriptions)
  • JAMB Registration numbers and candidate details (for manual JAMB processing)
  • Processed documents securely uploaded and delivered to you

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for operational and business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing your BRYT Pay account.
  • Processing transactions and funding your wallet.
  • Delivering purchased digital services and fulfilling manual orders (like JAMB letters).
  • Providing customer support and resolving disputes.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, and abuse.
  • Maintaining secure transaction records as required by financial regulations.
  • Improving our platform's user experience.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. However, we must share specific, limited information with external service providers to fulfill your requests. These may include:

  • Payment Processors: To handle wallet funding securely.
  • Telecom & Utility Providers: To deliver airtime, data, or TV subscriptions to your designated numbers.
  • Service Fulfillment Partners: When processing manual requests (like WAEC or JAMB services).

4. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We implement appropriate organizational and technical measures, including:

  • Secure database infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Encrypted authentication protocols.
  • Secure server-side processing for all transactions.
  • Restricted administrative access to sensitive user documents and data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and transactional information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accurate financial records, resolve disputes, and comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Nigerian Data Protection laws, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations requiring us to retain financial records).
  • The right to file a complaint with our support team or applicable data protection authorities.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

BRYT Pay uses cookies and local storage (such as session tokens) exclusively to keep you securely logged in, remember your preferences, and ensure the platform functions correctly. We do not use intrusive third-party tracking cookies for targeted advertising.
