Frequently Asked Questions

General & Account

What is BRYT Pay?
BRYT Pay is a digital-services platform developed by BRYT Technologies. We provide a convenient wallet system allowing you to purchase airtime, data, TV subscriptions, and specialized manual services like WAEC and JAMB processing securely.
Is BRYT Pay a bank?
No, BRYT Pay is a digital utility and service payment platform, not a licensed bank or financial institution.
How do I create an account?
Click on the "Get Started" button on our homepage, provide your basic details, verify your account, and you can start funding your wallet immediately.

Wallet & Payments

What is the BRYT Pay wallet?
Your wallet is a secure digital balance where you can store funds to make instant, seamless payments for any service on the BRYT Pay platform.
How do I fund my wallet?
You can fund your wallet via our integrated secure payment processor (Flutterwave) using bank transfers, USSD, or debit cards.
What happens if my payment succeeds but my wallet is not credited?
Don't panic! Sometimes network delays prevent immediate crediting. Please contact our support team with your payment receipt or reference, and we will manually verify and credit your wallet.

JAMB & WAEC Services

Is BRYT Pay an official JAMB website?
No. BRYT Pay is not JAMB's official website and we are not officially affiliated with the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board. We simply offer processing and assistance services to retrieve your documents.
What does JAMB Admission Letter Processing mean?
It means our administrative desk agents will take the details you submit, manually interface with official channels on your behalf, process the required fees, and deliver the final digital document directly to your BRYT Pay account.
Where will I receive my completed document?
Once marked as "Processed" by our admin team, your document (PDF or Image) will be securely uploaded to your dashboard. You can download it directly to your phone or computer.

Transactions & Security

What do the different transaction statuses mean?
Pending: Transaction initiated but awaiting payment or action.
Processing: A manual service (like JAMB) is currently being worked on by our desk.
Successful/Processed: Value has been delivered or document uploaded.
Failed: The transaction could not be completed.
Will BRYT Pay ever ask for my password?
No. Our support team will never ask for your account password. If someone claiming to be from BRYT Pay asks for your password, please report them immediately.
