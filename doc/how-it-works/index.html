How It Works

From creating an account to securing your digital documents, BRYT Pay makes the process incredibly simple.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up securely on our platform. Once logged in, you will have access to your personal dashboard and digital wallet where you can manage all your transactions.

2

Fund Your Wallet

Before purchasing services, add funds to your BRYT Pay wallet. We support secure transfers processed by robust payment infrastructure so you can top up easily and safely.

3

Choose a Service & Confirm

Select from our array of available services directly on your dashboard. Double-check your inputs (like phone numbers, smartcard IUCs, or Registration numbers) and verify the amount before completing the payment.

4

How Each Service is Processed

Our delivery times depend on the exact type of service you select:

  • Instant Delivery: Routine services like Airtime, Data bundles, Education PINs, and TV Subscriptions are completely automated. They are processed and delivered in seconds.
  • Express Processing (3-5 mins): NIN Certification slips (Regular, Standard, Premium) are processed and delivered to you within 3 to 5 minutes.
  • Standard Processing (15-30 mins): JAMB services (Admission letters, Original Results, etc.) and Digital Exam Certifications (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB) require secure administrative routing. These are processed by our desk within 15 to 30 minutes.
5

Completion & Document Delivery

Once your JAMB, NIN, or Exam Certificate order is successfully completed by our desk, the official PDF document is securely dispatched directly to your Email address and WhatsApp number.

You can also track your order status in real-time and instantly download the document straight from your BRYT Pay dashboard at any time!
