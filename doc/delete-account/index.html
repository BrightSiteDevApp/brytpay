Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your BRYT Pay account and associated personal information.

At BRYT Pay, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information.

If you no longer wish to use BRYT Pay, you can request permanent deletion of your account.

How to Delete Your Account

Send an email to brytpay@gmail.com with the following details:

Email Subject: BRYT Pay — Account Deletion Request

Information Required:

  • Your BRYT Pay Account ID.
  • Your registered email address.
  • Your reason for deleting your account (optional).

Our support team may verify your account ownership before processing your request.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

Verified account-deletion requests are processed within 3 days after successful verification, subject to any legal requirements or unresolved account matters.

What Information Will Be Deleted?

After your request is approved and processed:

  • Your BRYT Pay account will be permanently closed.
  • Your login access will be removed.
  • Personal profile information that is no longer required will be deleted.
  • Associated service documents and personal information will be deleted where legally permissible.

Some financial and transaction records may be retained where required by applicable law, regulatory obligations, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

Before Requesting Deletion

Please ensure that:

  • You have withdrawn or settled any remaining wallet balance.
  • You have resolved pending transfers or transactions.
  • You have downloaded any documents you wish to keep.

If you need assistance with any outstanding wallet balance or transaction, contact our support team.
